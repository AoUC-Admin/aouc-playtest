The Varkesh Holds

Agriculture, horses, hill towns and mounted border companies.

War stance
Divided over restitution through negotiation or conquest

The Varkesh Holds extend across agricultural valleys, hill towns, grasslands and lake basins southeast of the other inland realms. Grain, horses and mounted border companies give the Holds practical importance in peace and war.

King Rhuzan Terv holds an elected life-crown. This political tradition is distinct from the other crowns and does not make the realm’s internal interests uniform.

The empire promised restitution for earlier land seizures. Its collapse leaves those obligations uncertain. Some interests still seek recovery through contracts and negotiation; others see force as the only promise likely to be honored. A single settled allegiance in the wider war has not been established.

Lore and future scope; not yet playable

A predominant ancestry describes a realm’s population and political traditions, not the character or allegiance of every person. All five realms contain minorities, migrants and dissenting factions. Planar-Touch people live across the continent.
