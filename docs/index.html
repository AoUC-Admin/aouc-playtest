Your company. Your convictions.

A kingdom is more than its throne.

Begin with the people of Bellwether. Follow the campaign, take local work or set out along roads made dangerous by war and ambition.

01 / THE ROAD

Choose your obligations.

Find work, investigate trouble and decide which promises are worth keeping. The main campaign begins when you choose to join it.

02 / THE COMPANY

Give them a reason to stay.

Build a company of up to five. Companions have their own convictions, and what they witness can strengthen trust or test their loyalty.

03 / THE FIGHT

Read the battlefield.

Position your company on a hex battlefield. Balance movement, weapon attacks, spells, supplies and defense as each turn unfolds.

The Charterlands

One realm.
Many loyalties.

Travel between settlements with different needs, rival factions and people who remember your actions. A welcome in one town does not guarantee a welcome in the next.

The reconstructed campaign, twenty walkable settlements and current dialogue systems form the 0.10.0 alpha. Long-session playtesting continues.

Existing released alpha

The Dungeon Map Update

  • The Dungeon Map Update: 32 world dungeons with 90 floors, plus the newly painted Bellwether vault. Three dungeon discoveries tie into the main story.
  • Eleven unique outdoor maps bring the copse, black pool, shrines, standing stones, quarries and wrecks to life.
  • Walk through painted paths and floors, take stairs between levels, and return from combat to your saved exploration position.
  • Spell tooltips show readable effects and damage with your current character bonuses. Hover individual spells in the combat selector to compare them.
  • Tester campaigns begin with normal level-1 character creation. Boosted party starts and developer resources are reserved for the creator source launcher.
  • Expanded hover help throughout the interface, with preserved contextual item, ability and action explanations.
