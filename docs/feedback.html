Useful reports

Include game version, steps, expected and actual behavior, and Windows/GPU details when relevant. Mark campaign spoilers clearly. Reports request investigation; they do not promise adoption of a feature.

Keep personal data private

Reports are public. Do not attach personal files, credentials or unreviewed saves/logs. Crop screenshots and arrange an appropriate private transfer with the developer if a save is needed.

A GitHub account is required to submit or comment. The website is freely accessible. Please keep tester download links out of public reports.
