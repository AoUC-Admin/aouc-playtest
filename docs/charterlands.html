The Charterlands form the current playable realm. Its roads and rivers carry more than armies: they carry food, debt, testimony and the news by which distant towns judge one another. Bellwether is the company’s opening hub; Morrowtide is the royal capital.

Hadran’s betrayal and death left no settled authority able to bind the realm together. Courts, wardens, guilds and garrisons compete to govern. A town’s immediate need may be safe passage or grain, even while claimants argue over who deserves its loyalty.

Elven intervention and rival Human ambitions do not create a simple division between good and evil peoples. Communities and individuals face different pressures. The company’s reputation can change locally before news reaches a province or the wider realm.

The five Charterlands provinces are subdivisions of this one realm. They are not the five realms shown on the continental map. The game’s current campaign takes place here; the other four lands provide wider history and future production scope.

The current playable realm

A predominant ancestry describes a realm’s population and political traditions, not the character or allegiance of every person. All five realms contain minorities, migrants and dissenting factions. Planar-Touch people live across the continent.
