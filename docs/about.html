The Age of the Uncrowning is a single-player high-fantasy RPG in active development for Windows PC.

Development

The game uses Godot. Its current narrative presentation uses Dialogic, alongside authored dialogue and locally generated conversations constrained by game-owned rules. Development includes AI-assisted content; this preview is not a final store listing.

Images and features

The artwork and descriptions here represent development material. Testing continues, and a public release date has not been announced.

Website privacy

This static preview contains no analytics, advertising scripts, account forms or third-party embeds. A future hosting provider may process ordinary web access logs.

Current scope

The alpha focuses on the Charterlands, a five-person company, progression to level 30 and a voluntary three-act campaign. Four further realms are not playable in this build. Deeper population routines, faction plans and selected interiors remain continuing development.

Credits and notices

Godot, Dialogic, the inference runtime and model retain their own license notices in the download. AI-assisted art and content are development material; public testing does not establish commercial clearance. No full commercial release date is promised.
