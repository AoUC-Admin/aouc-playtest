The road ahead.

Target windows are estimates for planning and may change after tester feedback. They are not promised release dates. A milestone is complete only when its acceptance criteria pass.

October 2026

0.9.0 — Public Alpha

Release preparation

  • Promote the current narrative and gameplay line to mainline.
  • Deliver a self-contained Windows test, public guide, complete recorded update history and feedback tracker.

Acceptance: Exact package integrity, startup, campaign/save regression and shared-link download verification.

October–November 2026 target

Stabilization and player feedback

Next

  • Prioritize reproducible crashes, blocked progress and save problems.
  • Review settlement navigation, readable choices, companion behavior, combat/economy pacing and local dialogue on more PCs.

Acceptance: Resolve blocking reports and record repeatable fixes; measure actual playthrough and hardware results.

November–December 2026 planning window

Deeper settlement and relationship work

Planned; scope reviewed after alpha feedback

  • Expand the approved population, relationship and faction-plan foundations in bounded increments.
  • Develop selected interiors and settlement activities; define contracts and tests before adding new interactions.

Acceptance: Each increment has explicit scope, persistent consequences, save/reload coverage and ordinary playtest feedback.

After stabilization; date not committed

Campaign polish and release preparation

Before 1.0

  • Complete long-session campaign, literary, economy and difficulty review.
  • Add the tutorial after the interface settles; review accessibility, resolutions and hardware behavior.
  • Review content and dependency rights, attribution and actual release contents.

Acceptance: The agreed full-game acceptance criteria pass. 1.0 is not automatic after 0.9, nor a percentage-complete claim.

No date announced

Beyond the Charterlands

Future direction

  • The Ithral Reach, Basalt Covenant, Varkesh Holds and Saffron Expanse have lore roles but are not four additional playable maps today.
  • Each realm requires its own scoped production plan. Future stories and expanded activities are not promises for the current alpha.

Acceptance: Separate scope, production plan and creator approval before development commitments.
