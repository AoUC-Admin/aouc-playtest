The Ithral Reach lies east of the Charterlands, beyond forested ridges. Lake cities and cultivated terraces reflect a realm shaped by long habitation. The imperial seat, Lethar’s Confluence, lies within the Reach but has its own administration.

Emperor Ithariel’s murder has made grief a continental political force. King Caelir Senath must answer it while protecting his realm’s trade and people. Elven forces have crossed into the Charterlands, but neither the purpose nor the conduct of every commander is identical.

The demand for justice can become a demand that ordinary neighbors pay for a king’s crime. Some leaders press intervention further; others fear what vengeance will cost their soldiers, civilians and shared future. An Elf’s ancestry does not determine which position they hold.

Lore and future scope; not yet playable

A predominant ancestry describes a realm’s population and political traditions, not the character or allegiance of every person. All five realms contain minorities, migrants and dissenting factions. Planar-Touch people live across the continent.
