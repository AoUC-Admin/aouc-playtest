War stance
Armed neutrality under pressure; arms interests supply both sides

The Basalt Covenant occupies the northern mountain arc. Mines and passes make its decisions consequential far beyond its borders. The movement of iron, labor and supplies connects mountain prosperity to communities below.

King Dovren Keld’s crown operates within charters governing mines, water and labor. Those obligations matter alongside royal authority. The realm’s name should not be mistaken for proof that every house or interest acts with one purpose.

Dwarven arms houses sell to both sides of the looming conflict. Closing the passes might protect the Covenant, but would also hurt trading partners and reward smugglers. Profit, contractual duty and fear of a wider war pull its interests in different directions.

Lore and future scope; not yet playable

A predominant ancestry describes a realm’s population and political traditions, not the character or allegiance of every person. All five realms contain minorities, migrants and dissenting factions. Planar-Touch people live across the continent.
